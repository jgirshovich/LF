How to run it in the classroom
  1. Try it on one device first. This prototype puts the stage and the controls on the same screen, so you can test every move, the funny voice, and the lip sync on a single iPad or MacBook.
  2. On the big board: open this page, tap Stage only, then Full screen. Giggles fills the board with no buttons in the way.
  3. Voice: tap Start microphone and allow mic access. Your voice comes out of the same device's speaker in the funny voice you picked, and the louder you talk, the wider Giggles' mouth opens.
  4. Two screens: this prototype doesn't link two devices yet — that's the next step. It needs a free realtime go-between (like Firebase) so the iPad's taps reach the board in under a second. Until then, one device drives everything.

Keyboard shortcuts (handy on the MacBook): 1 wave, 2 jump, 3 laugh, 4/5 point, 6 smile, 7 dance, 8 confused, 9 sleepy, 0 surprise.

If the voice squeals: move the speaker away from the iPad, turn the speaker down a little, or hold the iPad mic close and speak toward it.
